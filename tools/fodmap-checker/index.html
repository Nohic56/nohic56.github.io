IBS / FODMAP Barcode Checker

Scan a product, upload a barcode photo, or type the number manually. Product data comes from Open Food Facts.

Spaces and hyphens are removed automatically.

Hold the barcode steady, fill most of the frame, and use good lighting.

Crop closely around the barcode when possible. A sharp, straight photo works best.

This is a screening aid, not a medical diagnosis or a definitive low-FODMAP certification. FODMAP effects depend on serving size, formulation, and individual tolerance. Open Food Facts is community-maintained, so ingredient information may be incomplete or outdated.
